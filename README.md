# CSE Specialization Guide — AI vs Software Development vs Network & Communication vs Embedded Systems & Robotics

A data-driven comparison of the four major CSE specializations, built for students choosing their track. Every data point is sourced from live, verifiable online sources as of 2026. Where reliable data could not be found, this is stated explicitly rather than guessed.

**Note on data:** Salary and market-size figures vary significantly across research firms and salary-tracking platforms due to differing methodologies. Every figure below is presented as a range, with a note on how much sources agree or disagree. Treat all numbers as directional guidance, not precise facts.

**Currency:** All salary figures are converted to USD for easy cross-region comparison.

## How to Use This Guide

This guide is organized by major, and each major is broken into sub-domains (specific career tracks within that major). For each sub-domain, you will find:

- Historical, current, and forecasted market size and growth
- Salary ranges across 8+ regions (Bangladesh, India, USA, UK, Germany, UAE/Gulf, Singapore, China, Canada, and others where relevant)
- Job opportunity and remote/onsite trends
- Top employers by region
- Required tools and technologies
- Required skills and abilities
- Trade-offs and limitations
- Risks (including automation risk)

Jump to any major using the Table of Contents above. If you only want a quick decision, skip to the [Master Comparison Tables](#master-comparison-tables) at the end.

## Sources & Data Reliability Notes

- Market-size figures for the same category often vary by 2–3x across research firms (e.g., different vendors reporting the same year's AI market at $347bn vs $900bn) due to differing definitions of market scope. All figures here are ranges, not single "true" numbers.
- Salary data is drawn from platforms including Glassdoor, Indeed, Levels.fyi, ZipRecruiter, PayScale, and industry salary-guide blogs. Bangladesh-specific data is often based on very small sample sizes (sometimes under 10 submissions) — treat with extra caution.
- US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) occupation growth projections (2024–34) are US-specific and used as the best available structured reference; they do not map perfectly onto Bangladesh's labor market.
- Where no reliable data could be found for a region/sub-domain combination after searching, this is marked as "Reliable data not available" rather than estimated.
- This guide reflects data collected in 2026 and will become outdated over time — treat as a snapshot, not a permanent reference.
